---
title: "Artalk"
description: "连接 Artalk 服务与站点标识。"
---

## 配置

```yaml
params:
  solitude:
    comment:
      use: artalk
      lazyload: true
      count: false
    artalk:
      server: https://comments.example.com
      site: My Blog
      option: {}
```

`site` 必须与 Artalk 后台一致。覆盖 CDN 资源时应保持脚本和样式版本兼容。

需要评论的页面应设置 `comment: true`。同时验证页面直达和反复 PJAX 切换。
