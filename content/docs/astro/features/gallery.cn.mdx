---
title: "图库"
description: "在 MDX 页面中组合图片。"
---

创建 `src/content/pages/gallery.mdx`，写入标题与图库：

```yaml
---
title: Gallery
---
```



```mdx
<Gallery columns={2}>
<GalleryItem src="/img/demo/cover-getting-started-v2.webp" alt="Create" />
<GalleryItem src="/img/demo/cover-pagination-v2.webp" alt="Collect" />
</Gallery>
```

`Gallery` 设置列数，`GalleryItem` 设置图片和替代文本，`GalleryGroup` 创建图库入口。添加 `/gallery/` 菜单后即可访问。
