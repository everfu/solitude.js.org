## Does a template copy publish my site?

No. Connect a host or upload `dist/`. The check workflow only validates the project.

## Why are comments or music missing?

Configure a global provider and enable the feature. Post `comment: true` does not enable a provider. The music hall and capsule are independent; the template capsule contains a sample playlist.

## How do drafts differ from hidden posts?

`draft: true` is development-only. `home: false` hides homepage areas while keeping direct access, search, and feeds. Future dates do not schedule publication.

## Why does language switching leave some text unchanged?

`locale` controls built-in UI only. Translate posts, menus, and datasets yourself.

## Why is my page absent from the menu?

Pages and navigation are independent. Add an entry to `menus`.
