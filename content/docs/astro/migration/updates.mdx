---
title: "Update the template"
description: "Keep your content while integrating upstream changes."
---

Template copies do not synchronize automatically and are not independently upgradeable npm themes.

1. Commit and back up configuration, posts, data, and styles.
2. Compare upstream theme and dependency changes on a separate branch; integrate the changes you need.
3. Check, build, and preview. Review content and old URLs before publishing.

Template and upstream histories may be independent; do not force unrelated histories together.

```sh
pnpm check
pnpm build
pnpm preview
```
